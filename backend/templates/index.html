How it works

Set up once. Go live in seconds after that.

Everything happens through the desktop app — no separate uploads, no browser tab to babysit.

One

Add a face

A clear photo is enough to start. Build a small library of looks you can switch between mid-call.

Two

Pick a live model

Edit, restyle, drive an avatar, or sync a mouth to your voice — four live models, one per session.

Three

Select it as your camera

camifyai registers as a webcam device on your machine. Pick it in Zoom, Meet, Teams — you're on.

Under the hood

Made to sit quietly underneath a real call.

Streamed, not rendered

Frames go out as they're generated. No export step, no processing screen between you and the call.

A real virtual webcam

Once running, camifyai is a camera source at the OS level — any app that opens a webcam can open camifyai.

Pay by the second

Credits deduct while you're actually streaming, in ten-second heartbeats. Close the call and billing stops with it.

Watermarked by default

Every stream carries a visible watermark unless the account has paid the one-time fee to remove it — origin stays traceable.

Usage you can audit

Every session lands in a usage log with the model, duration and credits spent — nothing shows up as a surprise.

Pay however works for you

Top up with a card via Flutterwave or with crypto — the checkout flow is the same either way.

Credits, not subscriptions

Buy a pack. Burn it when you stream.

One credit is one second of live streaming across any model. Credits don't expire.

Use it on yourself, or with permission.

camifyai is built for calls, streams and content where everyone involved knows a live AI camera is being used. Don't upload someone else's likeness without their consent, and don't use the app to impersonate a real person, mislead someone about who they're talking to, or produce sexual, harassing, or defamatory content of any real individual.

camifyai must not be used for any illegal purpose — including fraud, identity theft, evading a platform's ban, or generating content involving minors. Every stream carries a watermark by default so origin stays traceable, and accounts found violating this are suspended and may be reported to the relevant authorities.
